# Ink Wash Landscape - Interactive Digital Art

An immersive, interface-free interactive ink wash landscape experience that combines traditional Chinese painting aesthetics with modern digital interactivity.

## Experience

Open `index.html` in a web browser to enter a meditative journey through a living ink wash painting.

### Interactions

**Gentle Touch (Single Click)**
- Click anywhere to drop green ink
- Life emerges: trees grow, birds perch, people appear
- Each element appears as a black silhouette with a green ink halo

**Accumulated Force (Rapid Clicking)**
- Click repeatedly in the same area (5+ times quickly)
- Ink accumulates and bursts into cascading waterfalls
- Mountains fracture, trees topple, birds fall, people tumble
- The torrent flows downward and forms pools at the bottom

**Contemplative View (Right Click)**
- Right-click on vibrant areas to zoom in and focus
- Surroundings dim as you magnify the scene
- Observe intricate details: tree bark textures, bird feathers, garment folds
- Drag to explore the landscape while zoomed
- Right-click again to smoothly return to the full view

**The Cycle**
- Pools expand with ripples
- Fallen elements sink into the pools and vanish
- Pools slowly fade, leaving faint historical traces
- The landscape remembers but moves forward

## Aesthetic

- Pure black, white, and green palette
- No UI elements, text, or buttons
- Only ink, life, and the space between
- A meditation on creation, destruction, and moderation

## Technical

- Pure HTML5 Canvas and Vanilla JavaScript
- No dependencies or frameworks
- Responsive to any screen size
- Optimized animation loop

## Philosophy

This is not a game or an application. It is a digital landscape—explorable, inhabitable, and alive. Every interaction is a dialogue between restraint and excess, between observation and participation.

Like traditional ink wash painting, it embraces simplicity and negative space. Like life itself, it cycles through creation and destruction, leaving only traces of what was.
